Detailed Description

Plans routes on a road network with the A* algorithm.

The search runs on the streets (the dual graph), so that forbidden turns and the U-turn rule of RoadNetwork::isTurnAllowed are honoured. A route ends with the goal street, entered by an allowed turn: it reaches the end of it. The router holds a compact copy of the network's topology and of the street costs: it must be rebuilt (see build) whenever streets are added, opened or closed, or their turns or speeds change. Route queries are const and thread-safe, as each thread searches with its own buffers; building and taking snapshots are not.

The heuristic is the straight-line distance to the goal street's target node, scaled by the lowest cost per unit of distance over all streets, which makes it admissible and consistent for any non-negative costs and any coordinate system. Without coordinates on every node touching a street the heuristic is zero, i.e. the search is Dijkstra's.

With "datt" (the detour-avoiding travel time, see setEdgeWeight) every street also costs distanceCost · length. With path randomness (see setPathRandomness) each agent perceives each street cost multiplied by its own log-normal noise (see noise), so that agents with the same trip spread over more routes, whatever the weight.

Get a street cost function by name.

Parameters
nameOne of:
  • "traveltime": the street's estimated travel time plus the time needed to clear its exit queues, i.e. estimatedTravelTime() + nExitingAgents(ANY, true) / min(1, transportCapacity())
  • "length": the street's length
  • "uniform": 1 for every street
  • "datt" (detour-avoiding travel time): "traveltime" plus distanceCost · length
  • any other value is used as the name of a numeric custom attribute of the streets
distanceCostThe cost of a unit of length of "datt", in time per unit of length (s/m): 0.1 makes a metre cost as much as driving it at 10 m/s
Returns
